## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering** student based in Sri Lanka, specializing in **Industrial Automation and Control Systems**, with a strong background in **C++** and **AI**. 

- 🏭 My primary focus is on **PLC Programming, HMI/SCADA development, and Motor Control**.
- 🧠 Passionate about intersecting traditional automation with **AI, Computer Vision (OpenCV, YOLO), and Edge Computing**.
- 🔌 Experienced in hardware-software integration, bringing IoT and smart monitoring into modern engineering solutions.
- 📫 Reach me directly via **[nadhun0270@gmail.com](mailto:nadhun0270@gmail.com)**
